Selfie Aura Cam

Professional beauty filters & real-time photo editing.

Selfie Aura Cam combines real-time camera filters, GPU-accelerated beauty processing and detailed photo editing controls in one streamlined camera experience. Preview your look live, capture your photo, or edit images from your gallery with precise beauty and colour controls.

Main Features

Built for responsive live preview, high-quality photo capture and detailed control over filters and beauty effects.

✨

Real-Time Beauty Filters

Preview beauty effects and colour filters directly on the live camera feed before capturing your photo.

⚡

GPU-Accelerated Processing

GPU-based image processing delivers responsive real-time filtering while preserving detailed beauty and colour adjustments.

🎨

Curated Filter Collection

Choose from distinctive colour styles ranging from clean and natural looks to cinematic, cool, warm and expressive creative filters.

💎

Advanced Beauty Controls

Fine-tune facial beauty effects while retaining important natural details such as eyes, eyebrows, lips, hair and facial features.

💄

Lip Colour & Lip Liner

Personalise lip colour and lip-liner styling with dedicated colour and intensity controls for compatible beauty filters.

✦

Teeth Whitening

Enable or disable teeth whitening as part of the beauty settings for a cleaner, brighter smile when desired.

📷

HD & SD Capture Quality

Select between HD and SD capture modes depending on whether you prefer full-resolution processing or a faster rendered capture workflow.

🖼️

Gallery Photo Editing

Import an existing photo from your gallery and apply filters and supported beauty adjustments without taking a new picture.

✂️

Photo Cropping

Crop imported photos before editing so the final composition can be prepared directly within the app workflow.

🔄

Front & Rear Camera

Switch between supported front and rear cameras while keeping the camera interface focused on quick photo creation.

⚡

Camera Flash Controls

Access flash controls directly from the camera interface when supported by the active camera.

🔒

Local Photo Processing

Camera and beauty processing is performed on the device, with captured and edited photos saved through the local device media workflow.

Designed for live preview and captured-photo consistency. Selfie Aura Cam combines camera processing, beauty masks, colour grading and saved-photo processing while providing dedicated controls for capture quality and beauty customisation.
